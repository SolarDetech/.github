# SolarDetech

**SolarDetech platformu, GES'ler için panel bazlı dijital ikiz ve varlık yönetim platformudur. Termal denetimi tam otonom yapar.**

The SolarDetech platform is a panel-level digital twin and asset management platform for solar plants. Its thermal inspection is fully autonomous: orthomosaic, panel segmentation, 17-class anomaly classification and the report are produced from drone data without human intervention.
